# Try ToneScript in five minutes

## Listen and follow

Open `outputs/review/review-A.html` from the extracted review packet. It works without an installation. Read the legend, then play the trumpet reference while following the four draft gestures. Repeat with Choice, where drums and bass have separate rows.

Choose one moment in each recording. Write its time and describe the sound character in your own words. Does the corresponding mark communicate what you hear? A useful answer can be “no,” with an explanation of what is missing. These are automatic suggestions, not a completed musical interpretation.

## Compare and change

Play X and Y after the reference. Choose which better preserves its character, or say there is no clear preference. Explain what you heard before opening the facilitator key. Then compare Before and After and describe the change without looking up its intended direction.

To try an edit yourself, start the editor with `npm start` and open `http://127.0.0.1:4173/`. Choose the trumpet draft, load its matching reference, and select a gesture. Switch Playback version between Original reference and Score reconstruction. Increase Brightness / end, play the score again, then Undo. Note whether the change agrees with your expectation; changing a synthesizer parameter is not proof that the musical meaning is intuitive.

Use Listening note to explain a correction. Mark Reviewed by listening only after you actually listen. Save score retains the notes and correction history. Original recordings are not embedded in the score file. Imported or recovered scores have Reset disabled; Undo remains available for changes made in the current session.

## Tell us what would help a musician

Could you use a mark to explain a gesture to another player, rehearse a change of tone, or plan a sound? Try describing one actionable instruction. Also name a confusing mark or a quality the system cannot express. Exact imitation of the recording is not required.

Record your observations in `outputs/review/RESPONSE-SHEET.md` or on paper. Keep your own observations separate from a classmate's. For a second listener, use review-B.html to reverse X/Y order. The pages do not collect or transmit responses. Formal timed-recognition tasks still need target moments chosen during pilot listening before anyone is scored.

## What remains for submission

Use the listening results to correct both applied scores and save a short explicitly hand-authored fragment for each excerpt. Keep the same legend and retain the original drafts for comparison. The final approximately 1,000-word reflection should explain your design choices, specific successes and failures, and what you learned from listening. The existing reflection scaffold has prompts for those experiences; it is not a finished essay.
